# Nodra

> Knowledge before capital.

Nodra is an open education and gaming platform focused on blockchain, DeFi and digital finance.

Our goal is simple:

- Teach everything for free.
- Help people apply that knowledge in the real world.
- Build an open community around digital finance.

---

## Products

### 📚 Nodra Academy

A free learning platform designed to help anyone understand blockchain from the ground up.

Current learning paths:

- Blockchain
- Wallets
- Networks
- Digital Assets
- DeFi
- Lending
- Yield
- Security
- On-chain
- Real World

### 🎮 NDQuest

Nodra's game platform: quizzes, party games and live activations built for events, communities and creators. Sponsor-brandable, with white-label theming and question packs per company.

Live games:

- **Quest Drop** — pick a difficulty, open an envelope, answer to reveal a prize.
- **Time Attack** — race the clock, right answers add time, wrong answers take it away.
- **Show Down** — everyone answers together live, speed rewards more points.
- **Tap Rush** — race to a target, endless tapping, or team tug of war.
- **Roulette** — gather names (import, QR/code, or paste a list), spin, draw a winner.

### ℹ️ About

The public-facing page explaining Nodra's vision, roadmap and what's live across both products.

## Philosophy

We believe education should be accessible.

Knowledge comes before capital.

Instead of selling expensive courses, Nodra focuses on providing free, high-quality educational content while offering personalized guidance for people who want help applying that knowledge.
