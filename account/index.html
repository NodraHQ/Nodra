Image size guide

Every shape crops your image differently to fit its outline. Keep your main subject (logo, face, symbol) inside the dashed "safe zone" below for each shape - anything outside it may get cut off. The image itself is never resized or distorted, it always fills the shape completely (cropping the edges as needed), so proportion matters more than exact pixel size.

Recommended: a square PNG, at least 500×500px (800×800px or larger looks sharper, especially on high-resolution screens).

Classic 50% × 50% ≈250×250px on a 500×500 upload
Rounded 56% × 50% ≈280×250px on a 500×500 upload
Hexagon 70% × 60% ≈350×300px on a 500×500 upload
Coin 80% × 60% ≈400×300px on a 500×500 upload
Square 100% × 100% Whole image is kept, no cropping
Circle 71% × 71% ≈355×355px on a 500×500 upload

Percentages are relative to your source image's own width and height - keep your subject centered within that inner proportion for any image size. Coin is the most forgiving shape; Classic and Rounded crop the most from top and bottom (they taper to a point), so keep tall content centered and avoid placing anything important within the top/bottom 25%.
